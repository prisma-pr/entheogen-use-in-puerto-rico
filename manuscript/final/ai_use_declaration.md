# Generative AI use declaration

**Where this goes in the submission:** Taylor & Francis's generative-AI policy requires the declaration to
name the specific tool and version, describe how and why it was used, and place it in the Methods or
Acknowledgments section (or a dedicated AI-use statement section if the journal names one — JPD's own
instructions page does not name a specific section, only that a declaration is required). `v7_draft.tex`
already carries a `Generative AI use declaration` section (after Data availability, before the
bibliography), which is the natural home for this — the text below is a fuller, more specific
replacement for that section, using the wording/placement rules confirmed live for T&F (see
`manuscript/JOURNAL_REQUIREMENTS_VERIFIED.md`). **This file does not itself edit `v7_draft.tex`** — the
manuscript body is final and not to be edited (see the drafting brief) — so the more complete wording
below is the one to substitute in at submission time (a mechanical, disclosure-text change, not a
narrative edit), or to use verbatim in the ScholarOne AI-declaration metadata field if the platform asks
for one separately from the manuscript text.

## Declaration text

> Generative AI tooling (Claude, Anthropic, various model versions used iteratively over the course of the
> project between 2026-07 and 2026-09) was used in three ways in the preparation of this article. First, it
> assisted in developing and iterating the Python analysis pipeline (`analysis/` in the project repository:
> data loading and XLSForm-based decoding, missingness decomposition, attention-check and duplicate
> screening, nonparametric statistical tests, and figure generation) under the authors' specification of
> every analytic decision — role classification, the primary outcome and exposure, the four prespecified
> Benjamini–Hochberg test families, the cell-size and non-response conventions, and the exclusion criteria
> for the analytic sample. Second, it assisted in drafting and revising the manuscript text across multiple
> rounds, incorporating author-provided facts, decisions, and edits (see `manuscript/drafts/revision_notes.md`
> for the round-by-round record) and structured author feedback from three peer-review rounds
> (`manuscript/PEER_REVIEW.md`, `PEER_REVIEW_R2.md`, `PEER_REVIEW_R3.md`). Third, it assisted with
> literature search and citation verification during manuscript development. All statistical results were
> generated by an executable pipeline that reads the raw survey export directly rather than from
> AI-generated or hand-entered figures, all analytic decisions were specified by the named authors, and all
> manuscript content, citations, and numerical claims were reviewed and verified by the named authors
> against the source data before submission. The authors take full responsibility for the accuracy,
> integrity, and originality of the content of this article. No generative AI tool is listed as an author,
> and none was used to generate the underlying survey data.

## Why this wording, not boilerplate

- The three uses named (analysis-code development, manuscript drafting/revision, literature-search support)
  are exactly what the task brief specified and what `revision_notes.md` documents actually happened — no
  claim is made here (e.g., "AI-generated figures" or "AI-conducted statistical inference") that the project
  record does not support.
- "All analytic decisions were specified by the authors" is accurate: `analysis/README.md`'s "Key modeling
  decisions (locked with client)" section and the CLAUDE.md's "Confirmed analysis decisions" section both
  show the substantive choices (ordinal treatment of the outcome, retention of `pna`/`not_sure` as a
  category, dual-role handling, cell-size suppression threshold) were made and locked by the research team,
  not by the tool.
- The declaration explicitly states AI was **not** used to generate survey data or list an AI tool as an
  author, addressing the two things T&F's policy is most emphatic about.

## What was not verified

- No JPD-specific override to the general T&F generative-AI policy was found (see
  `manuscript/JOURNAL_REQUIREMENTS_VERIFIED.md`); this wording follows the general T&F policy rather than a
  JPD-specific template, because none exists on the pages reached.
