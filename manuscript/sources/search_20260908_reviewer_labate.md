Dr. Beatriz (Bia) Caiuby Labate is an anthropologist and leading researcher in psychedelic plant medicines, drug policy, shamanism, and ritual studies, with a strong focus on Latin America and sacred plant communities. 

### Current Institutional Affiliations:
- **Executive Director**: Chacruna Institute for Psychedelic Plant Medicines
- **Senior Advisor for Culture and Strategy**: Multidisciplinary Association for Psychedelic Studies (MAPS)
- **Adjunct Faculty**: East-West Psychology Program at the California Institute of Integral Studies (CIIS)
- **Visiting Scholar**: Graduate Theological Union (GTU) in Berkeley & Naropa University’s Center for Psychedelic Studies
- **Co-Founder & Site Editor**: Interdisciplinary Group for Psychoactive Studies (NEIP) in Brazil
