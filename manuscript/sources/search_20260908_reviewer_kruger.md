Daniel J. Kruger is affiliated with the University of Michigan, including the Population Studies Center, the Anesthesiology Department at the University of Michigan Medical School, and the Michigan Psychedelic Center. His contact email address is kruger@umich.edu. His psychedelic and harm reduction research focuses on understanding naturalistic psychedelic use, evaluating sources of information and trust among users, and developing harm-reduction resources to facilitate safe psychedelic experiences outside of regulated clinical settings.
