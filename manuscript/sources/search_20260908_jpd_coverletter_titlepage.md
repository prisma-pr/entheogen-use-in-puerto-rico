### Key Submission Requirements for the *Journal of Psychoactive Drugs* (via ScholarOne Manuscripts)

#### 1. Cover Letter
- **Access & Privacy**: The cover letter is uploaded during the ScholarOne submission process and is accessible only to the Editor-in-Chief and Associate Editors; it is not shared with reviewers.
- **Content Requirements**: Authors should use the cover letter to state the type of submission (e.g., regular manuscript or a contribution to a Special Section) and include any required administrative declarations or special instructions for the editors.

#### 2. Suggested Reviewers
- **ScholarOne Field**: Authors are prompted to submit contact details and affiliations for potential qualified reviewers during the step-by-step submission workflow on ScholarOne Manuscripts.
- **Selection Criteria**: Suggested reviewers should be experts in the relevant field of psychoactive drugs, substance use, or pharmacology and should not present a conflict of interest with any of the manuscript's authors.

#### 3. Title Page
- **Anonymization**: To support the journal's peer-review process, the main manuscript file should be blinded. Complete author details (full names, affiliations, contact information, ORCIDs, acknowledgments, and funding statements) are provided on a separate Title Page file or entered directly into the ScholarOne manuscript metadata fields.

#### 4. Running Head Requirement
- **Short Title**: A short version of the manuscript title (running head) must be provided in the metadata section during Step 1 (Type, Title, & Abstract) on ScholarOne Manuscripts.
- **Format**: The running head is used for headers on published article pages and should be a concise summary of the full title.
