## Naturalistic/community psychedelic surveys with comparable descriptive profiles
Queries: PubMed `("Puerto Rico") AND (ayahuasca OR psilocybin OR entheogen OR psychedelic OR
ceremonial)` (reused from 2a, surfaced `teixeira2026ayahuasca` as a byproduct); WebSearch `"psilocybin
retreat naturalistic setting global survey rise popularity 2020s review article"`; PubMed
`"Ceremonial Ayahuasca in Amazonian Retreats"`; PubMed `"Naturalistic psilocybin use is associated with
persisting improvements in mental health"`; PubMed `"self-treatment with lysergic acid diethylamide and
psilocybin mushrooms"`.

Six benchmarking sources gathered, spanning ayahuasca- and psilocybin-specific naturalistic cohorts:
`teixeira2026ayahuasca` (JPD 2026, n=203 ayahuasca ceremony attenders, Portugal — closest design analog
found anywhere in this search, published in the target journal), `pagni2025longterm` (JPD 2025,
naturalistic ayahuasca long-term outcomes), `ruffell2021ceremonial` (Frontiers in Psychiatry 2021,
six-month naturalistic ayahuasca retreat cohort with mental-health and epigenetic outcomes),
`nayak2023naturalistic` (Frontiers in Psychiatry 2023, prospective longitudinal naturalistic psilocybin
survey), `kopra2023investigation` (Global Drug Survey 2020 sub-analysis, self-treatment patterns),
`robinson2026field` (Global Psychedelic Survey 2023 qualitative sub-study, Contemporary Drug Problems
2026). These give demographic, setting, and satisfaction-ceiling benchmarks for the Discussion's
comparison of this sample's profile against other naturalistic cohorts.

## Adverse events and boundary violations in ceremonial and guided settings
Queries: WebSearch `"sexual misconduct boundary violations ayahuasca ceremony psychedelic facilitator
abuse survey"`.
Two sources: `kruger2025psychedelic` (Psychedelic Medicine 2025 — survey-based study of therapist sexual
misconduct and other adverse experiences among naturalistic psychedelic users; the closest published
evidence base against which this study's 5/67 non-consensual-contact finding can be contextualized) and
`peluso2020reflections` (Journal of Psychedelic Studies 2020 — reflections on the Chacruna Institute's
Ayahuasca Community Guide for the Awareness of Sexual Abuse, documenting the field's own harm-reduction
response to this problem). Numerous non-academic sources (Chacruna guide itself, addiction-recovery blogs,
travel-safety articles) were found but excluded as non-peer-reviewed; the two entries above are the
citable evidence base.

## Ceiling effects in self-reported psychedelic experience measures
Queries: WebSearch `"ceiling effect self-report satisfaction scale psychedelic experience questionnaire
measurement"`; PubMed `"Effects of Naturalistic Psychedelic Use on Depression, Anxiety, and Well-Being"`;
PubMed `"Measuring the subjective: revisiting the psychometric properties"`.
Primary source: `bouso2016measuring` (Human Psychopharmacology 2016 — psychometric properties of the
principal self-report rating scales used to assess acute hallucinogen effects, including discussion of
distributional/ceiling issues). This directly supports the manuscript's §4.2 measurement argument about
`ceremony_good`'s 51/64 ceiling at 5–6. A second candidate (PMC2225402, a general inpatient-satisfaction
ceiling-effect methods paper, non-psychedelic) was considered and excluded as off-topic once a
psychedelic-specific source was confirmed.
